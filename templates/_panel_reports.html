Fair price calculator

Fair price = grams/100 x (price per 100 g at quality 5 + extra per quality point x (quality - 5)) + packaging. Prices outside 40% to 200% of fair are pulled back into that band.

Making cost calculator

Making cost = grams/100 x cost per 100 g (interpolated between the quality levels in your sheet, localisation adding its share) + packaging per pack. Delivery cost per pack is extra: see the channel cost report. Fixed costs are not in this number.
